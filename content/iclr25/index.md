The [International Conference on Learning Representations 2025](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2025) was a major conference that took place in Singapore from April 24th 2025 to April 28th 2025. I had the privilege to represent RAiD at this conference, listening in to many of the major talks and sharings given across the first 4 days.

Here's a few notes I took from the various talks at the workshop:
- [**Building Safe and Robust AI Systems**](safe-robust-ai.md) by Prof Zico Kolter @ CMU
- [**Towards Building Safe and Secure AI: Lessons & Open Challenges**](safe-secure-ai.md) by Prof Dawn Song @ UC Berkeley
- [**Superintelligent Agents Pose Catastrophic Risks: Can Scientist AI Offer a Safer Path?**](scientist-ai.md) by Prof Yoshua Bengio @ University of Montreal

Note: **many** of the talks were indeed covered at the [Large Model Safety Workshop 2025](events/lmxsafety25).
