title: AI Engineer Singapore 2026
---
The AI Engineer Singapore Conference 2026 was a locally-organised conference by 65labs, a local grassroots organisation for AI education and literacy in Singapore, that took place from May 15th 2026 to May 17th 2026. As part of my work at Pragnition Labs, I had the opportunity to attend this conference over the last 2 days, listening in to many of the major talks and sharings given from various industry experts.

Many of the speakers spoke on various robotics and physical AI concepts, ranging from Sim2Real and Agent-based Robot Control to World Modelling and how we might contextualise the taxonomy of input-output schema of robotics subsystems.
